About Alcova

Alcova was founded in Lisbon in 2019 by three former rental operators who were tired of reconciling calendars by hand.

In 2025 Alcova joined the RentalBridge group. As a RentalBridge company we bring RentalBridge's distribution network and booking technology to operators in Portugal and Spain, while keeping our own team, our Lisbon office and our local-language support.
